SALATHE FRAMEWORK

A compact state.A complete crystal context.

Research overview

Molecular crystal generation must coordinate conformation, lattice geometry and periodic packing. SALA uses flow matching to evolve a compact, symmetry-constrained asymmetric-unit state while reasoning over the full periodic environment. This state–context separation enables joint generation across four chemical classes and all seven crystal systems represented in a 799-crystal held-out benchmark.

SALASymmetry-Aware Lattice and Asymmetric-unit generation

SALA's approach

Separate what needs to evolve from what the model needs to see. Symmetry connects the two.

Evolve the independent stateGenerate only the asymmetric-unit coordinates and symmetry-compatible lattice parameters.

Reason over the full contextExpand the full cell and model periodic interactions with a structure-aware transformer.

Return to symmetryMap coordinated predictions back to the independent state through OrbitPullback.

Three quantities, one decision

Molecular crystal generation must coordinate conformation, lattice geometry and periodic packing.

Conformation

The heavy-atom geometry of the molecules inside the asymmetric unit.

Lattice geometry

Cell parameters that remain compatible with the target symmetry.

Periodic packing

How symmetry copies and neighbouring cells arrange in the full periodic environment.
